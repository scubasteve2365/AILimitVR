# AI Limit VR 1.0.3 update ⚔️🥽
**Free mod • OpenXR • Native stereo**

A big fix-up for everything past the sewers:

- **Both eyes match.** Buildings and shadows no longer look different in each eye outside the sewers.
- **No more vanishing ground.** The ground and props used to pop in only when you were right next to them. New **Detail distance** setting in the menu: **Full** (default) matches the flat game, **Medium** and **Short** save a little CPU.
- **Much lower CPU load.** About 25% less CPU time per frame in busy outdoor areas.
- **Lock-on marker and item names** show at any distance.
- **Honest frame reporting.** Late frames now show up in fpsVR and SteamVR's own stats, so you can tune your settings.

**Tip:** resolution and DLSS only change GPU load. If fpsVR shows missed frames with your GPU well under budget, lower the game's shadow quality or set Detail distance to Medium.

**Update:** unzip, run `Install AILimitVR.bat` and type `1`. It updates in place and keeps your settings.

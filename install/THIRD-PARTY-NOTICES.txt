AI Limit VR includes these parts. Their licences are in the licences folder.

OpenXR loader 1.1.65 (Khronos Group), unmodified
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK-Source
  Apache-2.0.

MinHook (Tsuda Kageyu), compiled into AILimitVRNative.dll
  https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook
  BSD 2-Clause. Includes Hacker Disassembler Engine 64 (Vyacheslav Patkov), BSD 2-Clause.

Dear ImGui 1.93.0 (Omar Cornut), compiled into AILimitVRNative.dll, draws the settings menu
  https://github.com/ocornut/imgui
  MIT.

The OpenXR side (own XR thread, shared picture ring, flat panel layers) and the winhttp.dll proxy come from
Ori & the Will of the Wisps VR and Cuphead VR by the same author (MIT).

The menu text uses Georgia from Windows' own font folder. No font files are included.

NVIDIA DLSS is the game's own (it ships with AI LIMIT); the mod only switches it on for each eye.

AI LIMIT belongs to Sense Games and CE-Asia. This mod contains no game files.

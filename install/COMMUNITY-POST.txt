# AI Limit VR 1.0 ⚔️🥽
**Free mod • OpenXR • Native stereo**

AI LIMIT in true stereo VR. Both eyes are drawn from the same game tick: no alternate-eye shimmer, no warping. Your headset sits at the game's third person camera, so you look around the ruined city while you fight, seated with a gamepad.

## What it does
- Native stereo through OpenXR, with **NVIDIA DLSS in each eye**
- **World scale** and **decoupled pitch**: the world stays level and you look up and down with your head (toggle in the menu)
- HUD, lock-on orb and the game's menus on a panel, fixed in the room or following your head
- Holds your headset's refresh rate at normal game speed (checked at 72, 90 and 120 Hz)
- VR-unfriendly effects off: film grain, bloom, motion blur, radial blur, ambient occlusion
- In-headset settings menu: hold both sticks (or press `Insert`)
- One bat file to install and uninstall. No game files are changed

## What you need
- AI LIMIT on Steam
- A PC VR headset with OpenXR: SteamVR, or Virtual Desktop's VDXR for Quest
- A gamepad, and an NVIDIA RTX card for DLSS (TAA works on others)

## Install
Unzip it, double click `Install AILimitVR.bat` and type `1`. Start SteamVR or connect Virtual Desktop, then start the game from Steam.

## Please read
- Tested on a **Pimax Dream Air** (SteamVR) and a **Meta Quest 3** (Virtual Desktop). Other headsets should work; reports welcome!
- The mod keeps the game offline, and your monitor shows nothing while VR runs.
